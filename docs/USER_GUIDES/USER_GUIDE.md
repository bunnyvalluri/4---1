# 👤 Candidate User Manual

Welcome to the CareerAI platform! This guide covers the complete candidate journey.

## 1. Getting Started
1. **Register**: Sign up at `/register` with your email and password.
2. **Profile Setup**: Complete your educational details, degree, graduation year, target roles, and bio at `/profile`.
3. **Skill Matrix**: Rate your current proficiency across technical and soft skills on a 1–5 scale at `/skills`.

## 2. Navigating the Platform
- **Dashboard (`/dashboard`)**: Summary view of your highest matching careers, top skill gaps, and active roadmap progress.
- **Recommendations (`/recommendations`)**: In-depth breakdown of suitable careers, matching vs missing skills, and market demand.
- **Roadmap (`/roadmap`)**: Your 6-month customized milestone plan with interactive task checkboxes.
- **Resume (`/resume`)**: Instant ATS evaluation, Google XYZ impact review, and keyword optimization.
- **AI Advisor (`/chat`)**: Chat with your personal career assistant for interview strategies and career transitions.
